## IHPRF3 (OMIM: 616900) — Phenotypes Present in ≥80% of Patients

Based on a thorough search of PubMed-indexed clinical cohort studies of IHPRF3 (TBCK-related disorder), the study meeting the criterion of ≥10 patients **with explicit per-phenotype frequency data** is:

**Zapata-Aldana et al. (2019)** — a systematic review and delineation of IHPRF3 covering the then-largest worldwide cohort of 33–35 patients. This paper reviewed 33 previously reported cases plus 2 new patients and was identified as the largest worldwide case series to date for TBCK-related disorders, providing the main clinical and radiological features with explicit frequencies.

The explicit phenotype frequencies from this series are reported in Sabanathan et al. 2023 (PMID: 36522252) citing Zapata-Aldana 2019 (PMID: 30103036) as the source cohort:

The largest worldwide case series identified: profound global developmental delay (100%, n=32/32) and severe hypotonia (88%, n=29/33) as the two features meeting the ≥80% threshold. More than 80% of published cases presented with severe hypotonia, which was frequently neonatal in onset.

### Important Notes and Caveats:

1. **Only two phenotypes meet the ≥80% threshold** in the qualifying cohort (Zapata-Aldana et al. 2019, PMID: 30103036, n=33–35).
