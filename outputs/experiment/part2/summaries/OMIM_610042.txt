## Important Preliminary Note on Scope

**OMIM:610042 (Pitt-Hopkins Like Syndrome 1 / PTHSL1)** is caused exclusively by biallelic (*homozygous or compound heterozygous*) pathogenic variants in **CNTNAP2** (also called CASPR2 deficiency disorder / CDFE syndrome). This is a very rare condition, and the total number of published patients across all studies is small (approximately 70–80 worldwide as of 2023–2024). Consequently:

- Studies meeting the **≥10 patient** threshold are very few.
- The two best qualifying cohort studies are:
  1. **Strauss et al., 2006** (PMID: 16571880) — 13 Amish patients

The **Smogavec et al., 2016** study (PMID: 27439707) had only 8 patients and does not meet the ≥10 threshold. Similarly, the Panza et al., 2023 (PMID: 37805811) cohort had only 7. These are excluded per your criteria.

## Study 1: Strauss et al., 2006 — *N Engl J Med* — PMID: **16571880**

Strauss et al. identified a homozygous 1-bp deletion in the CNTNAP2 gene in **13 Amish patients** from 10 sibships with PTHSL1. They reported children with **cortical dysplasia, focal epilepsy, relative macrocephaly, and diminished deep-tendon reflexes**; intractable focal seizures began in early childhood, after which **language regression, hyperactivity, impulsive and aggressive behavior, and mental retardation developed in all children**. During infancy, **all patients had mild gross motor delay** and subtle limitations in motor skills.
