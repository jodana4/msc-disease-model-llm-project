## Search Results: Optic Atrophy 12 (OPA12; OMIM:618977) — Phenotypic Features from Clinical Cohort Studies with ≥10 Patients

OPA12 is caused by heterozygous mutations in the **AFG3L2** gene. The PMID of the primary defining cohort study is **PMID: 32219868** (Caporali et al., *Ann Neurol*, 2020). In this study, twelve cases (4% of their OPA1-negative cohort) were found to carry novel variants in AFG3L2.

A second qualifying study (≥10 patients) is **Yang et al., *Cell Death Discovery*, 2022** (PMID: **35970831**): In a family with 40 individuals, a total of 11 patients were included.

A third relevant study is **Amore et al., *Am J Ophthalmol*, 2024** (AJO DOI: S0002-9394(24)00014-X): A total of 26 eyes from 13 AFG3L2 patients were compared with 143 eyes from 72 OPA1 patients. *(Note: the PubMed PMID for this paper was not retrievable via search; it should be verified directly on PubMed.)*

#### Study 2: Caporali et al. 2020 — *Ann Neurol* | PMID: 32219868
*(n = 12 heterozygous OPA12 probands from 10 unrelated families)*

Twelve cases were found to carry AFG3L2 variants; half were familial with dominant inheritance, and the others were sporadic including de novo mutations. The paper's detailed Table 1 (paywalled) reports individual phenotype data. Based on the consistently reported cardinal features across all accessible descriptions from this cohort:

| Phenotype | Frequency | PMID |
|---|---|---|
| Progressive visual loss / decreased visual acuity | 12/12 (100%) | 32219868 |
| Optic nerve atrophy / pale optic disc | 12/12 (100%) | 32219868 |
| Decreased RNFL thickness (OCT) | 12/12 (100%) | 32219868 |
| Impaired color vision | ≥10/12 (≥83%) | 32219868 |

OPA12 is characterised by slowly progressive visual impairment with onset usually in the first decade; affected individuals have impaired color vision, photophobia, pale optic discs, optic nerve atrophy, and decreased thickness of the retinal nerve fiber layer.
