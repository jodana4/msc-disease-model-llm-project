### Study 2: Jiang et al. (2018) — 20 patients
**PMID: 29696776**
*(Am J Med Genet A. 2018;176(6):1315–1326)*

Xia-Gibbs syndrome is a newly described disorder characterized by developmental delay, hypotonia, speech delay, sleep apnea, and seizures. Neurodevelopmental delay is a ubiquitous feature of this condition. Data from developmental milestones showed evidence for delayed speech and that males were more severely affected.

The abstract and full text of this paper do not report individual phenotype frequencies as explicit percentages in the publicly available data.
