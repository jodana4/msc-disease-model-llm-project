## Background Knowledge

Pitt-Hopkins like syndrome 1 (PTHSL1) is caused by mutations in the CNTNAP2 gene. It shares features with classic Pitt-Hopkins syndrome but is genetically distinct. Key features include severe intellectual disability, absent or severely limited speech, seizures, and autistic features.
