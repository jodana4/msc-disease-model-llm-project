Disease: Myasthenic syndrome, congenital 9 (OMIM:616325)
============================================================
I'll analyze Myasthenic syndrome, congenital 9 (CMS9), which is caused by mutations in the AGRN gene encoding agrin, a protein critical for neuromuscular junction formation and maintenance.

## STEP 1 & 2: Identify Hallmark Phenotypes with HPO Terms

From my training knowledge, CMS9 (AGRN-related) is characterized by:

1. **Muscular weakness** - generalized myasthenic weakness
2. **Fatigable weakness** - characteristic of all myasthenic syndromes
3. **Ptosis** - drooping eyelids, very common
4. **Respiratory insufficiency** - often prominent, can be life-threatening
5. **Hypotonia** - reduced muscle tone, especially in infancy
6. **Delayed motor development** - due to early weakness
7. **Ophthalmoparesis** - weakness of eye muscles
8. **Bulbar weakness/Dysphagia** - feeding difficulties
9. **Distal muscle weakness** - characteristic of AGRN mutations
