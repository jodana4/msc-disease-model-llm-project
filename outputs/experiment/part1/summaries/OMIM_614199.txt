## STEP 1 & 2 — IDENTIFY HALLMARK PHENOTYPES AND ASSIGN HPO TERMS

This disease is caused by mutations in the LAMB2 gene, which encodes laminin beta-2, a component of the glomerular basement membrane. It represents a spectrum that includes Pierson syndrome at the severe end.

Key hallmark phenotypes from my knowledge:

1. **Nephrotic syndrome** - The defining feature, present in essentially all patients
2. **Proteinuria** - Universal finding as part of nephrotic syndrome
3. **Focal segmental glomerulosclerosis (FSGS)** - Characteristic histological finding
4. **Steroid-resistant nephrotic syndrome** - Does not respond to standard steroid therapy
5. **Chronic kidney disease/End-stage renal disease** - Progressive renal failure is typical
6. **Ocular abnormalities** (in subset but very common) - including microcoria, lens abnormalities
