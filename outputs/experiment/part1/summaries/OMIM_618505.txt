## STEP 1 & 2 — IDENTIFY HALLMARK PHENOTYPES AND ASSIGN HPO TERMS

Stolerman neurodevelopmental syndrome is caused by heterozygous variants in the NSD2 gene (also known as WHSC1). From my knowledge, this is a recognizable syndrome with the following hallmark features:

**Core features (present in ≥80% of patients):**
- Global developmental delay
- Intellectual disability
- Speech delay/impairment
- Hypotonia
- Feeding difficulties in infancy
- Distinctive facial features (including telecanthus, downslanting palpebral fissures)
- Short stature/growth delay
