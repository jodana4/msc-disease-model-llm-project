## STEP 1 & 2: Identifying Hallmark Phenotypes with HPO Terms

Netherton syndrome is characterized by a classic triad of features, along with other highly penetrant phenotypes:

1. **Ichthyosis linearis circumflexa** - distinctive serpiginous, migratory skin lesions
2. **Trichorrhexis invaginata (bamboo hair)** - pathognomonic hair shaft abnormality
3. **Atopic diathesis** - atopic dermatitis, allergies, elevated IgE
4. **Congenital ichthyosiform erythroderma** - present at birth
5. **Failure to thrive** - common in infancy
6. **Recurrent infections** - due to skin barrier defect
7. **Hypernatremic dehydration** - in neonatal period
8. **Sparse/brittle hair** - scalp, eyebrows, eyelashes affected
