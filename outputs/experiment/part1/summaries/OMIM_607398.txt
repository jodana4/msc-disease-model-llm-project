## Background Knowledge

Glucocorticoid deficiency 2 (GD2) is caused by mutations in the MRAP gene (Melanocortin 2 Receptor Accessory Protein). This is a form of Familial Glucocorticoid Deficiency (FGD), characterized by isolated glucocorticoid deficiency with preserved mineralocorticoid function. MRAP is essential for proper trafficking and function of the ACTH receptor (MC2R).

---

## STEP 1-3: Identifying Hallmark Phenotypes

From my training knowledge, the hallmark features of GD2 (MRAP-related FGD) include:

1. **Adrenal insufficiency** - universal finding
2. **Hypocortisolemia** - defining feature
3. **Elevated ACTH** - characteristic biochemical finding
4. **Hyperpigmentation** - due to elevated ACTH/MSH, very common
5. **Hypoglycemia** - common presenting symptom, especially in infancy
6. **Failure to thrive** - frequent early manifestation
7. **Recurrent infections** - due to cortisol deficiency
