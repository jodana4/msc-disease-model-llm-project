## Background

This is an extremely rare autosomal recessive disorder caused by mutations in the BCKDK gene. BCKDK normally phosphorylates and inactivates the branched-chain keto acid dehydrogenase (BCKD) complex. When BCKDK is deficient, the BCKD complex is constitutively active, leading to excessive catabolism of branched-chain amino acids (BCAAs: leucine, isoleucine, valine), resulting in chronically low BCAA levels.
