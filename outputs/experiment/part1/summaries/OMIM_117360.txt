## STEP 1-2: Identify Hallmark Phenotypes with HPO Terms

SCA29 is caused by mutations in the ITPR1 gene and is characterized by congenital, nonprogressive cerebellar ataxia. From my knowledge, the hallmark features include:

1. **Cerebellar ataxia** - core feature present in essentially all patients
2. **Cerebellar hypoplasia/atrophy** - consistent neuroimaging finding
3. **Hypotonia** - present from infancy in most patients
4. **Motor delay** - delayed motor milestones very common
5. **Dysarthria** - speech difficulties due to cerebellar dysfunction
6. **Gait ataxia** - difficulty walking, often delayed ambulation
7. **Intellectual disability** (mild) - cognitive impairment in majority
