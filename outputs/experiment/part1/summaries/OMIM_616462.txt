## STEP 1 & 2 — IDENTIFY HALLMARK PHENOTYPES AND ASSIGN HPO TERMS

Acrofacial dysostosis, Cincinnati type is caused by mutations in the POLR1A gene. This is a rare mandibulofacial dysostosis syndrome with limb involvement. From my knowledge of this condition:

**Craniofacial features:**
- Micrognathia (small jaw)
- Malar hypoplasia (underdeveloped cheekbones)
- Downslanted palpebral fissures
- Cleft palate
- External ear abnormalities

**Limb features:**
- Preaxial limb anomalies (thumb/radial ray defects)
- Short stature
