## STEP 1 & 2: Identify Hallmark Phenotypes with HPO Terms

Holt-Oram syndrome is caused by mutations in TBX5 and is characterized by upper limb defects and cardiac abnormalities. From my knowledge:

**Upper Limb Abnormalities (virtually 100%):**
- Abnormality of the upper limb - present in essentially all patients
- Abnormality of the thumb - most common upper limb finding
- Triphalangeal thumb - very frequent
- Aplasia/hypoplasia of the radius - common finding
- Abnormality of the carpal bones - frequently affected

**Cardiac Abnormalities (~75-95%):**
- Atrial septal defect - most common cardiac defect (~50-80%)
- Abnormal heart morphology - present in vast majority
